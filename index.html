01 / THE PROBLEM

Indian roads don't
follow the script.

Lane markings disappear. Traffic mixes cars, buses, bikes, autos and animals. Potholes, parked vehicles and sudden crossings turn a predictable road into a dynamic environment.

Our planner continuously reads the scene, estimates risk in every direction and replans a safe path instead of assuming the road will behave like a controlled test track.

01Mixed trafficCars · buses · trucks · bikes · autos
02Animals & pedestriansCows · dogs · crossings · sudden movement
03Road damagePotholes · debris · uneven surfaces
04Blocked pathsParked vehicles · roadwork · lane closures
